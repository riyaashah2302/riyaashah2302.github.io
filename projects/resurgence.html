Resurgence

CategoryDesign
Project TypeFashion design collection
CollaborationIndividual
Focus Areas
Fashion DesignConcept DevelopmentGarment ConstructionVisual Storytelling

Overview

Resurgence is a fashion design collection that takes emotional experience as its design material. The collection explores the tension between fragility and strength — the way trauma reshapes us, and the forms that vulnerability and resilience produce when they coexist in the same body. Each garment in the collection is developed to hold that tension visually: structured where it must be, yielding where it can be, never entirely one or the other.

Challenge

Translating psychological experience into physical form — designing garments that communicate emotional states without becoming illustrative or literal.

Contribution

Concept development, mood research, garment design, pattern development, construction, and styling. All work produced independently.

Deliverables

Complete fashion design collection, concept board, mood research, technical garment development, final collection photography.

Concept

Resurgence begins with the question of what trauma leaves behind — not the wound, but the scar tissue, the altered posture, the ways the body reforms itself around an absence. The collection translates that process into silhouette, material, and construction logic.

Research and Mood

Mood research drew from medical photography, organic natural forms — shells, dried plants, healing tissue — and the history of fashion that has engaged with the body's interior logic. The research was not about literal representation but about finding formal analogues for emotional states.

Development

Garments were developed through material experimentation and pattern work that allowed structure and softness to coexist in the same piece. Seam placement, panel construction, and material selection were each used as tools for expressing the collection's central tension.

Final Collection

The completed collection presents each garment as a complete resolution of the design problem — neither fully protective nor fully exposed, each piece occupying the in-between space the concept requires.

Key Takeaway

Resurgence demonstrates Riya's ability to develop a coherent concept through rigorous research, illustration, and garment construction — translating abstract ideas into resolved, wearable work.
