Projects / Design / Until Forever

Until Forever

Category Design
Project Type Bridal collection
Collaboration Individual
Focus Areas
Fashion Design Bridal Wear Botanical Reference Illustration Garment Construction

Overview

Until Forever is a bridal collection built around softness as a structural principle. The collection takes botanical forms — the layered geometry of petals, the translucency of early-morning light through leaves — as its primary design vocabulary, translating them into garments that feel romantic without being conventional. The collection was developed through extensive illustration and material research before moving to pattern and construction, with each garment designed to feel as though it has grown into its final form rather than been imposed upon it.

Challenge

Bridal design has an established visual language that can be difficult to work within without reproducing it. The challenge was to make something unmistakably bridal that did not feel borrowed.

Contribution

Full design development from concept through illustration, pattern work, and final garment construction. All work produced independently.

Deliverables

Bridal collection concept board, botanical research, illustration series, technical garment development, final collection photography.

Concept

Until Forever takes its emotional premise from the idea that a wedding garment should feel like something that has always existed in the wearer's world — quiet, inevitable, already theirs. That feeling drove a design process oriented toward softness, botanical lightness, and the avoidance of anything ostentatious.

Research and Mood

Botanical references were central to the research phase — not as pattern motifs, but as structural logic. The way a petal holds its shape while remaining yielding; the way translucent layers create depth without opacity. Material research explored silk organza, fine lace, and layered chiffon in response to these references.

Illustration

Illustration was used as a primary design tool in this collection — not to document garments after design, but to develop them. The illustration series worked through silhouette, proportion, and fabric behavior before any patterns were cut.

Final Collection

The completed collection presents a series of bridal looks unified by their botanical softness and structural restraint. Each garment is designed to move with the wearer rather than around them.

Key Takeaway

Until Forever demonstrates Riya's ability to develop a coherent concept through rigorous research, illustration, and garment construction — translating abstract ideas into resolved, wearable work.
