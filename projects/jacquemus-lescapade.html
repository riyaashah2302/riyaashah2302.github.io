Jacquemus — L’Escapade Customer Journey

CategoryMarketing
Project TypeDigital marketing and customer journey strategy
CollaborationIndividual
Focus Areas
Customer Journey MappingSocial Media StrategyRetargetingLoyalty MarketingAR Concepts

Overview

Jacquemus built its cultural equity on sun-drenched social imagery and an intimate brand voice. The L’Escapade weekender bag extends that world into a new product category — one that requires a different customer conversation than the brand's signature mini bags. This project maps a full-funnel strategy: from initial social discovery through retargeting, product education, and an AR try-on concept, to post-purchase loyalty mechanics that deepen long-term engagement. Each phase is designed to feel like a natural extension of the Jacquemus aesthetic rather than a conventional retail funnel.

Challenge

A weekender bag represents a higher-consideration purchase than Jacquemus's usual impulse-friendly accessories. The strategy must educate, inspire, and convert without breaking the brand's effortless editorial tone.

Contribution

Developed the full-funnel strategy, customer journey mapping, channel recommendations, AR concept, and loyalty mechanics. All deliverables produced independently.

Deliverables

Customer journey map, channel-by-channel strategy deck, social content concepts, AR feature brief, loyalty mechanic framework, final presentation.

Context

Jacquemus has cultivated one of fashion's most enviable social communities — intimate, sun-soaked, and emotionally resonant. L’Escapade enters that world as a product with a different use case: a travel companion rather than an accessory accent. The strategy begins by understanding what that shift means for the customer relationship.

Campaign & Experience

The strategy organizes around three journey phases: Discovery (social-first storytelling, creator seeding), Consideration (retargeting, product education, an AR try-on concept that lets customers visualize the bag in their travel context), and Conversion and Loyalty (post-purchase content, community participation mechanics, and a referral program tied to the brand's escapade narrative).

Customer journey — Discovery phase concept

Consideration phase — retargeting and product education

Conversion and loyalty — AR and community mechanics

Channel Execution

Instagram and TikTok anchor Discovery, carrying the brand's signature visual language. Email and Meta retargeting serve Consideration with richer product storytelling. The AR concept is designed for integration within the Instagram shopping layer. Loyalty mechanics live in post-purchase email flows and a community content program.

Key Takeaway

This project demonstrates Riya's ability to move from consumer insight to strategic recommendation — developing a complete, channel-literate marketing strategy rooted in brand understanding and audience empathy.
