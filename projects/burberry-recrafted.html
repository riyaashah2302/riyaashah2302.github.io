Burberry — ReCrafted

Category Marketing
Project Type Circular luxury campaign
Collaboration Individual
Focus Areas
Circular Economy Brand Strategy Campaign Design Luxury Marketing Sustainability

Overview

Burberry's trench coat is one of fashion's most enduring icons — and one of its most personal. ReCrafted is a campaign and service concept that invites customers to bring their own materials to Burberry's ateliers to be incorporated into a new, bespoke trench coat. The proposal connects personalization, circularity, and craftsmanship into a single brand experience that deepens emotional ownership while reducing waste. The campaign is designed to feel entirely native to Burberry's heritage voice — never trend-chasing, always quietly exceptional.

Challenge

Luxury brands face growing pressure to demonstrate circularity authentically, without reducing heritage products to PR exercises. The challenge is to design an experience that is genuinely circular and genuinely Burberry.

Contribution

Developed the campaign concept, service design, creative direction, channel strategy, and visual narrative. All deliverables produced independently.

Deliverables

Campaign concept and brief, service design framework, creative direction document, digital channel plan, visual narrative deck.

Context

Circular luxury is no longer optional for major fashion houses — but it is easily done badly. The risk is an experience that feels like a discount program or a CSR obligation rather than a genuine extension of the brand's craft values.

Campaign & Experience

ReCrafted is structured around an in-store atelier appointment. Customers bring materials — a grandmother's scarf, a piece of family fabric — and Burberry's craftspeople incorporate them into a new, made-to-order trench coat. The service is documented and shared as a customer story, creating organic content that demonstrates the brand's craft values without feeling manufactured.

Channel Execution

The campaign launches with a hero film introducing the concept, supported by a customer-story series across Instagram and YouTube. Email invitations to existing customers drive appointment bookings. In-store and PR activations anchor the craftsmanship narrative.

Key Takeaway

This project demonstrates Riya's ability to move from consumer insight to strategic recommendation — developing a complete, channel-literate marketing strategy rooted in brand understanding and audience empathy.
