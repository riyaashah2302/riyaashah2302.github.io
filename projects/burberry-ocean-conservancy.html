Projects / Marketing / Burberry × Ocean Conservancy

Burberry × Ocean Conservancy

Category Marketing
Project Type Social awareness and CSR campaign
Collaboration Individual
Focus Areas
Social Media Strategy CSR Communication Sustainability Marketing Community Engagement

Overview

This campaign concept connects Burberry's British heritage and craftsmanship with the Ocean Conservancy's ocean advocacy mission, expressed through a social-first strategy built around recycled materials, community participation, and transparent sustainability communication. The proposal maps a full social channel plan — from Instagram and TikTok awareness content to community challenges, partner storytelling, and a recycled-material product tie-in — designed to demonstrate that Burberry's sustainability commitments are substantive rather than ornamental.

Challenge

Fashion brand CSR campaigns frequently feel disconnected from the brand's core identity, reducing them to noise. The challenge is to design a campaign that feels authentically Burberry and meaningfully connected to ocean advocacy.

Contribution

Developed the campaign concept, partner narrative, channel strategy, community mechanic, and visual direction. All deliverables produced independently.

Deliverables

Campaign concept brief, social channel plan, content series framework, community challenge mechanic, partnership narrative, measurement framework.

Context

Burberry's British heritage is inseparable from landscape — the coasts, the sea, the weather that made the trench coat necessary. That connection provides an authentic bridge to ocean advocacy that most fashion brands cannot claim.

Campaign & Experience

The campaign organizes around a community beach-clean challenge paired with a recycled-material product integration. Participants share their clean-up stories using a campaign hashtag; Burberry amplifies through its own channels and commits to a material or financial pledge for each post. The partner narrative with Ocean Conservancy provides independent credibility.

Channel Execution

Instagram carries the visual storytelling — craftsmanship imagery alongside ocean landscape content. TikTok anchors the community challenge mechanic. Email activates existing Burberry customers. The Ocean Conservancy partnership generates earned media and third-party credibility.

Campaign concept — craftsmanship and ocean advocacy

Community challenge mechanic — TikTok execution

Measurement

Proposed KPIs include campaign reach, community challenge participation, earned media value from partner amplification, and a sustainability perception tracker administered pre- and post-campaign. All figures are proposed targets, not reported outcomes.

Key Takeaway

This project demonstrates Riya's ability to move from consumer insight to strategic recommendation — developing a complete, channel-literate marketing strategy rooted in brand understanding and audience empathy.
